# Privacy policy

**Sidecar for Aside**, published by Thera North, Inc.
Last updated 2 August 2026.

## The short version

The app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and
no server of ours. Your data stays on your phone and on the computer you pair
with.

## What the app stores on your phone

- The name and network addresses of each computer you pair with.
- The pairing key that computer issued to your phone.
- Your preferences, such as the selected account and any custom paths.

This is kept in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes
it. You can also remove a computer at any time in Settings, which erases its key
from the phone.

## What the app sends, and where

The app talks only to the bridge on a computer you own, over your own network. It
sends the commands needed to list sessions, read transcripts, and pass on the
messages you type. Session content stays between your phone and your computer.

The app contacts no other service. It has no analytics and no crash reporting.

Tapping a link, such as the link to aside.com or to the source code, opens your
default browser. What happens after that is governed by that site's own policy.

## Camera

The camera is used for one purpose: to scan the pairing code your computer
displays. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted. Images are never saved.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.

## Third parties

Aside is separate software you license and run yourself, under its own terms and
privacy policy. This app is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with,
endorsed, or supported by its makers.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this file in the public
repository, with the date above updated.
